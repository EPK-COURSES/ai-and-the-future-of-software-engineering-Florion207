# Personal Reflection

Before researching this topic, I understood that software engineering required continuous learning, but I did not fully realize how much Artificial Intelligence is changing the profession. Through this research, I learned that AI is not simply another tool but a technology that is reshaping how software engineers work and develop their careers.

One thing that surprised me most was how quickly AI-powered tools have become part of everyday software development. Tools such as GitHub Copilot and ChatGPT can assist with coding, debugging, and documentation. While these tools can increase productivity, they also create new challenges because developers must verify the accuracy and quality of AI-generated outputs.

One concern I have is that some developers may become overly dependent on AI tools. If engineers rely too heavily on generated solutions without understanding the underlying concepts, their problem-solving abilities may weaken over time. I believe it is important to maintain strong knowledge of programming fundamentals even as AI becomes more advanced.

The research also highlighted many opportunities. AI can reduce repetitive work and allow engineers to focus on more complex tasks such as system design, architecture, and innovation. Engineers who understand both software development and AI technologies may have significant career advantages in the future.

As a Software Engineering student, this research reinforced the importance of continuous learning. Technology will continue to evolve throughout my career, so I must be prepared to learn new tools, frameworks, and development approaches. I plan to continue improving my programming skills, building projects, maintaining a portfolio, and learning how to use AI responsibly.

Overall, this research changed my perspective on career development in software engineering. Technical skills remain important, but adaptability, lifelong learning, and the ability to work alongside AI technologies will be equally important for future success.
